## Editorial notes (precision added beyond the literal decision text; flagged for the orchestrator)

1. **Normalization in step (i).** Applied literally, "(i) `w_{V,k} *= P_k(a | c)`; (ii) `q_k *= M_k`; marginal `r_S = sum_k q_k w_{S,k}`" counts `M_k` twice for the acting seat (once in `q_k`, once in the shrunken mass total), so the acting seat's marginal across branches is wrong: on T6 it gives villain `(0.1336, 0.0322)` (ratio 0.241) instead of the correct `(0.52, 0.22)` (ratio 0.4231 = `f_A P_A + f_B P_B`). The spec therefore writes (i) as `w_{V,k}[c] *= P_k(a | c) / M_k` (and the split as `w_{V,kX} = w_{V,k} P_k(X | c) / M_{kX}`), which keeps every seat's branch totals equal and puts the integrated likelihood in `q_k` exactly once; all posteriors and the marginal of every seat are then the exact Bayesian quantities, and the non-acting-seat posterior is `q_k / sum_j q_j` as the decision requires. This is the only change to the decision's formulas.
2. **Branch-level preflop stop.** "Its `q_k` and masses stay frozen" was applied at branch level (every seat's masses and the shared `q_k`), replacing revision 4's "that seat's branch `k` stops while other seats continue in `k`": with a shared `q_k` a per-seat stop that still let other seats multiply `q_k` would account evidence inconsistently, and a node absent at a prefix is absent at every longer prefix of a prefix-closed store, so the two readings coincide in practice.
3. **`log_reach` under shared weights.** Each seat's masses are rescaled after every observed action by that seat's own `max_c r_S[c]`; because `q` is shared, a seat's logged factor now falls after other seats' actions too (hero logs `ln(0.37)` in T6). It remains `<= 0` and is provenance only.
4. **`BranchResidual{cause: "cap"}`** is reported once with `seat: hero` (the share is seat-independent under the equal-total invariant); the `ApproxReason` type is unchanged.
