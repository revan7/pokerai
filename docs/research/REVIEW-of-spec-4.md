**P02 — EV-support repair RESOLVED; cross-actor continuation NOT FULLY RESOLVED.**

Spec §8.4 L520 now requires the identical mapped action and a normalized payoff in **every positive-posterior branch**, never renormalizes missing support away, and correctly makes the 0.2/0.8 example's raise EV unavailable. §§4.4/8.4 L225–226, L248–258, L520 represent known frequency mass plus `unresolved_mass`, suppress both headlines when unresolved mass is positive, and distinguish partial missing nodes from an entirely unavailable lookup. T7 at L708 agrees. The first two-actor split at L519/L707 is numerically correct: integrated masses 0.54/0.20 give receiving weights 0.7297/0.2703 without indexing villain's cards as hero's.

The remaining continuation gaps are confined to that same repair:

1. **Later evidence across existing histories:** L518 updates the acting seat on an on-menu action; L519 transfers evidence to other seats only when splitting an off-menu wager. It gives no receiving-seat update when a later on-menu action has different likelihoods in already-existing histories. For example, equal history weights and integrated likelihoods 0.9/0.1 require history weights 0.9/0.1 for an otherwise unconditioned receiving seat; the written rules leave its weights 0.5/0.5. Local child weights `q_X` also do not specify reweighting between different parents. Define the bounded approximation for these transitions, or explicitly invoke L554's fallback when this posterior is unavailable; a present source node alone does not establish it.
2. **Stop/resume contradiction:** L518 says stopped branches are not conditioned further and have no lookup node; §9.3 L554 says descendant conditioning stops. But L552 explicitly resumes the same seat at a later present preflop node. Node presence does not itself reconstruct missing translated history or justify the receiving posterior. State which stopping causes permit resumption, what proves the continuation valid, and when `stopped` is cleared; otherwise retain the stated fallback.
3. **Residual identity:** L519 permits each seat's residual to combine several shared histories, while §9.1 L534–536 gives every `Branch` exactly one `history: u8`. Once the acting seat has merged histories that another seat still retains, its per-history masses needed for L519's `M_X` are unavailable. Specify an aggregate residual identity and the affected continuation fallback; do not invent constituent masses. A general joint inference engine is unnecessary.
