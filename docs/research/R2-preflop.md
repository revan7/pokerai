**R2 — Preflop strategy sources for a local 6-max NLHE cash assistant**  
Research cutoff: 2026-09-10. Target hardware, software, response time and development budget are user-supplied constraints. This is a research report; no solver was installed or run, no account was created, and no strategy collection was downloaded in bulk. “Not verified” identifies a gap in the inspected evidence. Recommendations and proposed acceptance criteria below are engineering judgments, not measured results.

**Decision**

No source inspected establishes a complete, freely reusable, machine-readable strategy **and per-action EV** collection covering 6-max cash at 100, 150, 200 and 250bb, both straddle positions, and all requested multiway branches. The useful free materials are partial charts, simplified strategies, restricted viewers, or experimental source code. A verified free 250bb collection was not found. The most useful starting references are [RangeConverter’s 200bb charts](https://rangeconverter.com/articles/poker-charts-6-max-200bb-no-limit-texas-holdem), [FreeBetRange’s library and export documentation](https://help.freebetrange.com/Library/), and [GTOBase’s solution catalog](https://gtobase.com/); none establishes the entire requested deliverable with open data rights.

**Recommendation:** obtain an explicitly licensed offline strategy bundle with frequencies, action EVs and solve metadata; validate it before integration. Investigate PokerData first for ingestion fit, then a documented solver-output supplier for missing/custom configurations. PokerData exposes JSON strategy/EV documentation and advertises 100/150/200bb cash bundles and custom/bulk licensing. Its precise six-max cash solver provenance, complete branch coverage, 250bb/straddle delivery and permission for this live-use application are **not verified**. Therefore this is a procurement candidate, not an endorsed equilibrium dataset. [PokerData documentation](https://docs.pokerdata.io/nlhe/), [catalog and licensing inquiry](https://pokerdata.io/).

For a strict zero-cost/open-source baseline, use a clearly labeled approximate model plus individually verified chart references; a complete, credible GTO+EV product within two weeks is **not verified**. Current community implementations demonstrate that simplified six-player calculations can be fast, but their continuation models and validation are the limiting factors. [DCFR-SOLVER](https://github.com/exinori/DCFR-SOLVER), [GTOpen](https://github.com/MatthewPDingle/GTOpen).

**1. What “complete” must mean for this project**

For this report, completeness means every reachable decision in an explicitly specified preflop tree, not merely a chart for each seat. Required branches are: unopened pots; each legal opener/responder pair; opener responses to each 3-bettor; responses to 4-bets and jams; cold calls and cold 4-bets; raise-plus-caller squeezes; opener and caller responses to squeezes; additional callers; and live-game limp/overlimp/isolation continuations. These are proposed acceptance requirements. Their practical significance is illustrated by a commercial pack that separately enumerates squeeze configurations and by HRC’s warning that rare/multiway lines converge later than opening ranges. [GTOsims pack contents](https://gtosims.com/products/100bb-6-max-cash-rake-nl200-pokerstars), [HRC convergence documentation](https://www.holdemresources.net/docs/monte-carlo-sampling/).

Keep three objects separate: a hand range arriving at a node; an action frequency conditional on that hand and history; and the EV of each available action. An export of range weights does not by itself establish action EVs or a complete decision tree. PokerData documents separate range/EV fields, while FreeBetRange documents Pio-style weighted range strings. [PokerData NLHE format](https://docs.pokerdata.io/nlhe/), [FreeBetRange export](https://help.freebetrange.com/Export/).

**5. Can we solve preflop ourselves on the specified machine?**

**Yes for an explicitly simplified research game; a production-quality six-max cash equilibrium with meaningful full-hand EV within the proposed schedule is not verified.** CFR can operate on a small preflop action tree whose leaves use showdown equity or a value model. The key limitation is the game solved: replacing postflop betting with showdown changes the value of calls, position and future investments. A fixed equity-realization factor introduces assumptions rather than recovering full postflop strategy. Concrete implementations expose exactly these choices. [DCFR-SOLVER preflop implementation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/exinori/DCFR-SOLVER/main/src/preflop.rs), [GTOpen architecture](https://github.com/MatthewPDingle/GTOpen), [HU equity-continuation example](https://github.com/chirenonhive/poker-solver).

Do not mistake open postflop software for a six-max preflop solver. [b-inary/postflop-solver](https://github.com/b-inary/postflop-solver) and [TexasSolver](https://github.com/bupticybee/TexasSolver) target postflop solving; neither inspected project establishes the requested full six-player preflop solution pipeline. Their usefulness would be in constructing/calibrating continuation values, which is additional research and does not itself solve multiway continuation.

**Compute calibration.** Original Pluribus trained a six-player blueprint over about **eight days on 64 cores**, approximately **12,400 CPU-core-hours**, using **less than 512 GB RAM**. That was a complete poker-agent research system, not a benchmark for a tiny equity-only preflop tree. It is useful evidence that “six-player poker is feasible” and “six-player poker is solved accurately on this desktop in seconds” are very different claims. [Brown and Sandholm, Science 2019](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aay2400).

No inspected source reports a validated, equivalent six-max 100/200/250bb raked solve on an i7-13700K/RTX 5080. A direct throughput extrapolation from Apple Silicon or an RTX 3090 is **not verified**. Proposed resource policy: benchmark one small tree first, record wall time, actual CPU utilization, host peak RSS, GPU peak allocation, build/export overhead and quality at multiple checkpoints; then expand depths and histories. The target machine plausibly accommodates small abstractions, but that is an inference from the community measurements, not a measured guarantee.

**Accuracy caveats that matter more than iteration rate:**

- Multiplayer CFR does not inherit the general two-player zero-sum guarantee that average strategies converge to a Nash equilibrium. Published research studies strong multiplayer performance despite that missing general guarantee. Six-max strategies should be described as solutions to an abstraction with measured diagnostics, not certified globally unexploitable NLHE. [Gibson, 2013](https://arxiv.org/abs/1305.0034).
- More postflop detail costs memory; reducing it can distort preflop hand selection. Simple Poker illustrates weighted flop subsets and bucketing, including a full-flop HU example requiring terabytes of memory. That example is not a RAM requirement for every six-max preflop model. [Flop subsets and bucketing](https://simplepoker.com/en/News/What_is_flops_subset__How_it_is_used_in_GTO_Solvers__What_is_bucketing__72).
- Small aggregate strategy changes do not establish that rare squeezes and multiway continuations are well sampled. HRC calls its convergence indicator a heuristic based on recent strategy changes; its default target can leave rare lines unconverged. [Convergence and subtree sampling](https://www.holdemresources.net/docs/monte-carlo-sampling/).
- Local best response is a diagnostic/lower-bound probe, not a full-game upper bound on exploitability. NoRegrets’ log documents cases where attractive HU-line results concealed much larger multiway losses. Benchmark both, and do not use wins against random/calling bots as a human-pool strength certificate. [Benchmark audit](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/conorarmstrong/noregrets/main/BASELINES.md).

**Proposed self-solve fallback:** start from the MIT six-player DCFR implementation only after auditing chip accounting and continuation semantics; make stacks, blind/straddle structure, rake and sizes explicit; retain all requested cold-call/squeeze responses. Use equity terminals for an initial experimental policy and later replace them with validated continuation values. Label its output “approximate preflop model EV.” GTOpen is an additional architecture/performance reference pending license clarification. Neither should be shipped as proven GTO cash advice on the strength of README benchmarks.

[Product](https://monkerware.com/solver.html), [FAQ](https://monkerware.com/faq.html). | No verified target-machine CPU-hours/RAM or universal “Monker means accurate” guarantee. A historic GTO Wizard configuration used **5% rake, 0.6bb cap**, 30/30/30 buckets; that is metadata for that particular library, not every Monker or current Wizard solution. [Historical solution methodology](https://blog.gtowizard.com/all-you-need-to-know-about-our-solutions/). |

**7. Live-game configuration and adjustments**

**Straddles: store the actual game, not a position relabeling.** A conventional UTG straddle changes preflop order and leaves the straddler last to act initially; it does not move the button or change postflop positional order. A Mississippi button straddle can start preflop action at SB. Rules are venue-specific: PokerStars Live’s published cash rules allow only an UTG straddle at twice the BB, demonstrating that button straddles cannot be assumed universally. [UTG-straddle analysis](https://blog.gtowizard.com/preflop-strategy-in-straddled-pots/), [button-straddle configurations](https://blog.gtowizard.com/watch-learn-replay-legendary-final-tables/), [PokerStars Live rules](https://www.pokerstarslive.com/poker/cashgamerules/).

For the standard six seats UTG/HJ/CO/BTN/SB/BB, proposed configuration examples are: UTG-straddle initial order HJ→CO→BTN→SB→BB→UTG; button-straddle order SB→BB→UTG→HJ→CO→BTN under the documented Mississippi convention. Confirm the room’s actual order and permitted amounts as input rather than inferring them. The unit conversion is exact only when **all** quantities and the full posting/action structure are preserved. This is an engineering derivation from the rules above.

Example calculation: $2/$5/$10 with $1,000 stacks is 200 base bb but 100 straddle units. The posted pot is $17 = 1.7 straddle units. An ordinary game with 0.5/1 blinds starts with 1.5 units; the two games are not equivalent after merely halving stack depth. For equal base stacks 100/150/200/250bb and a 2bb straddle, normalized stacks become 50/75/100/125 straddles, with different forced-bet structure still retained. These figures are arithmetic examples, not quoted solver outputs; the additional blind/position effect is documented in the [straddle analysis](https://blog.gtowizard.com/preflop-strategy-in-straddled-pots/).

**Rake, caps and time collection.** Configure percentage, cap, cap units, pot eligibility, no-flop-no-drop, any preflop-rake rule and fixed drops separately. HRC distinguishes no-flop-no-drop, full rake and GG-style preflop cases, and recommends unraked chipEV for an action-independent time charge. It does not natively model every jackpot-drop structure. [Cash/rake documentation](https://www.holdemresources.net/docs/cashgame/).

Proposed treatment: if a fixed time fee is already payable independently of the current action, subtract it in session profit accounting rather than changing every hand’s action comparison. If collection is taken from particular pots or winners, model that actual rule. Express dollar caps in the same units as the strategy. Do not map a live high-rake game to “NL50” based on stake labels alone. For example, 5% capped at 0.5bb and 5% capped at 1.37bb are distinct inspected datasets; neither establishes a match to a particular casino. [PokerData catalog](https://pokerdata.io/), [GTOsims specification](https://gtosims.com/products/100bb-6-max-cash-rake-nl200-pokerstars).

**Large live opens and very deep stacks.** A 2.5bb-open online tree does not directly describe a 4–6bb live open. Published comparisons show that rake, raise size, position and depth jointly change preflop responses; deep 4-bet strategy also differs from 100bb strategy. Therefore do not uniformly widen all hands at 200bb or automatically carry over 100bb stack-off decisions. [Large preflop raises](https://blog.gtowizard.com/how-to-respond-to-large-preflop-raises-in-poker/), [deep IP 4-betting](https://blog.gtowizard.com/ip-4-betting-in-deep-stacked-cash-games/).

Proposed input model: retain every dealt player’s stack, committed chips, position and history. For a particular HU confrontation, the smaller remaining stack limits mutual further investment, but a multiway node can have different effective stacks and side pots against different opponents; preserve the vector instead of one global “effective stack.” HRC’s configurable stacks/multiway support and the original multiplayer poker formulation supply the relevant model context. [HRC cash setup](https://www.holdemresources.net/docs/cashgame/), [Pluribus](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aay2400). Exact 250bb action frequencies for the target game remain **not verified** until data are acquired or solved.

**Nine-handed tables playing short.** Distinguish six players actually dealt cards from nine dealt with three already folded. In the first case, label positions relative to the current button and active dealt seats. In the second, earlier folds convey card-distribution information; deleting those players’ history ignores bunching. A nine-handed hand reaching six remaining players is therefore not automatically the six-handed starting distribution. [Bunching/card-removal explanation](https://blog.gtowizard.com/a-beginners-guide-to-poker-combinatorics/). Proposed UI fields: seats occupied, seats dealt, sitting-out status, button/blinds/straddles and all actions from the beginning of the hand.

**Population tendencies: qualitative live advice is better established than representative numerical priors.**

| Tendency | Evidence and limitations | Proposed adjustment to test, not a universal rule |
|---|---|---|

It also explains circumstances where overlimping can be reasonable, so “all limping is a leak” is not supported. This is coaching evidence, not a random sample of low-stakes casinos. [10 Laws](https://upswingpoker.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/10-Laws-of-Live-Poker-2023.pdf). | Against a demonstrated tight 3-/4-bettor, remove unsupported bluff combinations from the opponent model and recompute responses; do not simply use the same calling range because the pot odds look attractive. |

The Hand2Note live database currently advertises **1,039,274 hands, 37,942 players and 19 channels**, drawing from public streams and mixing game formats. That is valuable observable live data, but not a representative census of casino $1/$2–$5/$10 NLHE; televised selection and the TAG filter limit extrapolation. [Database scope](https://www.hand2note.com/LivePokerDatabase). The SmartEV 30M-hand study is explicitly online, so it should not be presented as a live-population measurement. [SmartEV guide](https://help.freebetrange.com/guides/Smart_Preflop_Ranges_For_6-max_Holdem.pdf).

Book reference: **Ed Miller, _The Course: Serious Hold ’Em Strategy for Smart Players_ (2015), ISBN 9781511768320** is a relevant live low-/mid-stakes strategy reference. Bibliographic details and an author interview are available; specific supporting page quotations were **not verified**, so this report does not attribute precise population percentages to the book. Recommendation: make exploitative profiles optional and evidence-based. Record opportunity counts, position and sizing; shrink sparse observations toward a documented prior. Evaluate on held-out sessions/players, not the hands used to set the profile. Treat “loose caller,” “tight 3-bettor” and “aggressive regular” as observable behavior models, not one blanket “live” switch. These are proposed statistical safeguards; no calibrated local-pool parameters were verified in this research.

**Suggested configuration priority, not a claim of available data:** first the user’s most common actual blind/rake/straddle setup; then normal 100/150/200/250bb; then each straddle structure with normalized depths calculated from the original stacks. Four depths × three posting structures × two rake profiles already gives 24 configurations before adding raise-size menus or unequal stacks. This arithmetic illustrates why the whole Cartesian product should not be the first-week target.

**Required machine-readable contract, proposed:**

- Game metadata: solver/version or commit; source/license; generation date; player count actually dealt; ordered positions; complete stack/post vector; antes/straddles/dead money; rake function; allowed action sizes and all-in rules; postflop abstraction; convergence diagnostics.
- Node identity: full public history including folds/calls and exact raise-to amounts; actor; pot, committed chips and remaining stacks; legal actions; reach weights; supported/missing/unreachable distinction. Use the provider’s documented action path, not a guessed token translation. [Example action-path specification](https://docs.pokerdata.io/action-paths/).
- Strategy: action probability conditional on hand and history, plus separate reaching range. Preserve original precision. A 169-class representation needs correct 6 pair / 4 suited / 12 offsuit combination weights when computing aggregate frequencies; do not average 169 cells equally. [Poker combinatorics](https://blog.gtowizard.com/a-beginners-guide-to-poker-combinatorics/).
- EV: each available action’s value, scoring coverage, units, reference point and continuation assumptions. PokerData’s small-blind units require explicit conversion; do not assume every supplier uses BB. Zero-weight actions may still have scored EVs, and unavailable EV must remain unavailable. [NLHE EV documentation](https://docs.pokerdata.io/nlhe/).

**Validation procedure, proposed:**

1. **Coverage:** traverse the declared bounded tree; check that every legal action either has a successor or a defined terminal. Audit a checklist of RFI, every legal position-pair defense, vs-3-bet, vs-4-bet/jam, cold 4-bet, raise+caller, multiple callers, squeeze responses and limp continuations. Enumerate omitted histories instead of representing them as folds. Rare-line convergence deserves separate attention. [HRC rare-line guidance](https://www.holdemresources.net/docs/monte-carlo-sampling/).
2. **Accounting and legality:** verify stacks/pot conservation including rake, uncalled returns and side pots; check action order after straddles, minimum raises and short-all-in reopening under the selected rule set. Test explicit dollar-to-BB-to-straddle conversions. Match the venue’s rules rather than assuming a universal straddle convention. [Cash rules](https://www.pokerstarslive.com/poker/cashgamerules/), [rake/straddle modeling](https://www.holdemresources.net/docs/cashgame/).
3. **Probabilities and cards:** each supported hand’s action probabilities must be nonnegative and sum to one within serialization tolerance; exclude impossible card combinations; propagate reach weights consistently through the action history; test pair/suited/offsuit aggregation and folded-card assumptions. [Combinatorics and bunching](https://blog.gtowizard.com/a-beginners-guide-to-poker-combinatorics/).
4. **EV semantics:** independently reconcile fold, uncontested-win and all-in terminal examples. Check that strategy EV equals the action-frequency-weighted action EV under one common reference point. Distinguish sunk contributions from further investment; test SB/BB and currency factors. Frequencies alone cannot reconstruct the missing continuation payoffs. [Separate strategy/EV fields](https://docs.pokerdata.io/nlhe/), [equity-only continuation implementation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/exinori/DCFR-SOLVER/main/src/preflop.rs).
5. **Independent strategic comparison:** select representative common nodes and rare/deep/straddle/multiway nodes, and compare against an independently generated reference with matched stacks, rake, sizes and continuation model. Examine EV loss and gross range errors, not exact equality of every mixed frequency. A local best-response probe or stable chart is useful evidence but not a full six-player exploitability certificate. [Multiplayer CFR limitations](https://arxiv.org/abs/1305.0034), [Monker accuracy caveats](https://monkerware.com/faq.html).
6. **Self-solve checks:** retain multiple seeds/checkpoints; measure uncertainty and strategy drift by branch; include multiway probes and off-tree input cases. Record how often a lookup falls back and what value model is used there. Predefine acceptable errors after observing reference noise; no universal numeric “GTO accuracy” threshold was verified for the available multiplayer models. [NoRegrets benchmark limitations](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/conorarmstrong/noregrets/main/BASELINES.md).
7. **Runtime:** benchmark cold/warm offline lookups and normalization on the actual Windows machine with the network unavailable. Proposed target: preflop lookup should consume only a small fraction of the 5–15 second total response budget. This is an engineering expectation, not a measurement; the compact advertised cash bundles support testing a local cache before considering online solving. [Bundle sizes](https://pokerdata.io/).

**Recommended baseline boundary:** a local, versioned cache with exact licensed strategies for the most common game configurations; explicit unsupported-state handling; genuine per-action EV only where supplied or validated; separate approximate and exploitative modes. Acquire or commission missing 250bb and straddle trees rather than asserting that stack interpolation produces GTO. If complete free/open data is non-negotiable, scope the first release as an experimental study model: this audit did not verify a free complete strategy+EV collection capable of satisfying the entire live-game specification in one to two weeks.
