## R8 items incorporated

- Measured latencies replace the extrapolated ones (§7, §10.1): river 4 ms; turn 0.17 s (0.22 s to 0.3%); reduced flop 6.2 s AVX2 / 11.4 s SSE2 at 970 MB (510 MB i16, +25–30% time); full flop 42 s at 3.8 GB, 44 s at 2.0 GB in 16-bit mode; run-to-run variance 1.3–2.6×; budgets kept (2/6/10 s, 15 s hard) with stated headroom; the flop figures are flagged as measured with 179×264-hand ranges, store ranges unmeasured (V3, risk 3).
- AVX2 build requirement (§3.7): `.cargo/config.toml` `target-feature=+avx2`, `solver-worker/build.rs` check, `ready` reports `build_features` and `cpu_features`, engine refuses a non-AVX2 worker; FMA not required (none emitted).
- Pinned commit `9d1509fe5077d019825f833eed04b16d342dfda1` and the two vendored patches (bincode/bincode_derive `=2.0.0-rc.3`; `dangerous_implicit_autorefs` fix in `action_tree.rs` lines 393/396/408) in `PATCHES.md` (§3.7).
- API constraints (§3.7, §10.3): locks before `finalize()`, no solving after `finalize()` (re-solve from scratch), `solve_step` granularity 0.08–0.3 s with no in-iteration cancel (deadline/cancel checks between iterations, `compute_exploitability` ≈ one iteration), `cache_normalized_weights()` after every navigation, tree build 1–70 ms, allocation 0–10 ms, `finalize` 0.2–0.35 s, `memory_usage()` within 1.3% of peak, rake needs both rate and cap > 0.
- Threads: 16 kept as default (16 = 24 within noise; 8 threads 1.35× slower; memory-bandwidth bound) (§3.4).
- Bunching cost (×27–×40 on turn/river, flop not attempted) supports the S28 cut (§10.3).
- Pre-solver throughput planning (§10.5): 1,755 × 6.2 s ≈ 3.0 h per scenario (reduced tree), 1,755 × 42 s ≈ 20.5 h (full tree); tier-1 ≈ 12 h idle, all 24 scenarios ≈ 72 h.
- Template measured analogues (§10.1) and force-all-in 0 on the river (0.15 would replace the 0.75 bet by all-in at SPR 1).
- Toolchain facts (§3.6): active toolchain `x86_64-pc-windows-gnu`, MSVC target installed without C++ build tools, everything except Tauri builds on GNU.
- Upstream maintenance/unsafety facts added to risks (§15 rank 5).
